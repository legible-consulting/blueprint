# S1 A2 — Correct until sendable, and log it

Session 1 · 30 minutes · alone

## You need

- The chat from S1 A1.
- A blank page titled "Corrections log".

## Steps

1. Read the reply again.
2. Tell the assistant one thing to change, in plain words. One change per message.
3. Write the change on the log before you read the new reply.
4. Tag the change: voice, fact or relationship.
   - Voice: how it sounds.
   - Fact: something true or false about the business.
   - Relationship: what you may promise or say to this customer.
5. Repeat from step 2 until you would send the reply as it is.
6. Count the entries. Five or more is normal.
7. Mark the one entry that would have cost the most if it had reached the customer.

## Write down

- The log, with tags.
- The marked entry.

## Bring

- The log to session 2. It is the input for S2 A1.
