# S2 A4 — Three drafts, stated criteria

Session 2 · 30 minutes · pairs

## You need

- A chat window.
- The example's second request and its diary file. The paths are in `../example/README.md`.

## Steps

1. Before any draft, write three criteria for a good reply. One line each. At least one
   criterion is a fact the reply must get right.
2. Open a new chat. Paste the request and the diary.
3. Type: "Write three different replies to this customer. Number them."
4. Grade each reply against each criterion: pass or fail. Nine marks.
5. For each reply, write one correction and the criterion it fails.
6. Do not pick a winner.

## Write down

- The three criteria.
- The nine marks.
- The three corrections.

## Remember

- Criteria first. A criterion written after the drafts is a preference, not a test.
