# S5 A3 — Two versions of one fact

Session 5 · 15 minutes · pairs

## You need

- A fresh copy of `example/messy/`.
- The example's two price lists. The paths are in `../example/README.md`.

## Steps

1. Open both price lists. Find one item that differs.
2. Decide which list is current. Use the dates in the files, the flyer and the notes.
3. Make a folder named `archive/`.
4. Move the older list into it.
5. Write a line in the map: `archive/` holds old work and is never read.
6. Search the folder for the item from step 1. Count the files that still carry it.
7. Open a new session. Type the first request. Find the price it used.

## Write down

- The item and its two values.
- The file you kept and why.
- The count from step 6. It must be one.
- The price the new reply used.

## Do not

- Do not merge the two lists into a third file. That makes three homes.
