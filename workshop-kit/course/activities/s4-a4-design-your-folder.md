# S4 A4 — Design your folder

Session 4 · 35 minutes · alone, then pairs

## You need

- Your three sections from S4 A3.
- The copy of your real folder, open in your tool.
- `../walk-test.md`, items 1 and 2.

## Steps

1. Make the folders your piles need. Method in one place. Facts in one place. One folder per
   stage of work. One folder for outputs. One archive.
2. Move each file to its folder.
3. Write one stage contract as `CONTEXT.md` in one stage folder, with four parts:
   - Input: the files it reads, by path.
   - Do: the task, in plain words.
   - Output: the one file it writes, and where.
   - Human check: what a person looks at before the next stage runs.
4. Write the map at the top, on one screen: purpose, layout, naming, routing table. One row
   per job from S4 A3.
5. For each rule in the map, add why it exists and today's date.
6. Close the session. Open a new one on the folder.
7. Run walk test item 1. Your pair records what it read.
8. Run walk test item 2 with one real request. Your pair records the files opened.
9. For each fail, change the map. Run the item again.

## Write down

- The map.
- The contract.
- Items 1 and 2: pass or fail, with the evidence.

## Bring

- The folder to session 5.
