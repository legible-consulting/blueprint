# S5 A4 — Handover with the assistant off

Session 5 · 20 minutes · pairs

## You need

- Your pair's folder, on their machine or as a copy.
- The assistant closed. No chat open.

## Steps

1. Read the map of your pair's folder. Nothing else yet.
2. Choose one request from the routing table.
3. Do the request by hand: find the inputs the contract names, do the step, write the
   output where the contract says.
4. Each time you need to ask the owner something, write the question down instead. Do not
   ask yet.
5. Finish, or stop after fifteen minutes.
6. Read your questions to the owner.
7. The owner writes the answer to each question into the map or a contract.

## Write down

- The request you chose.
- Whether you finished.
- The questions, and where each answer went.

## Pass

- You finished from the map and the contracts alone.
