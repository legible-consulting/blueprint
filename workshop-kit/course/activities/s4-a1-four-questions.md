# S4 A1 — The four questions

Session 4 · 20 minutes · alone, then pairs

## You need

- The job you brought.
- Your three lines from session 1.

## Steps

1. Answer in writing, one line each:
   1. Who is this for?
   2. What must they do next, once they have it?
   3. Where does the information come from now? Name a place for each fact.
   4. What breaks when it goes wrong, and who feels it?
2. Read answer 2 again. It must name a decision or an action. "Read it" is not an answer.
3. Read answers 1 to 4 to your pair.
4. Ask your pair to repeat answer 2 back in their words.
5. Correct answer 2 until they can.

## Write down

- The four answers, final.
