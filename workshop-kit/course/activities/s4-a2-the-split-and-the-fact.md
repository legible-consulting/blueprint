# S4 A2 — The split and the fact

Session 4 · 20 minutes · pairs

## You need

- Your four answers from S4 A1.
- Your note from S1 A3: the fact and its home.

## Steps

1. Split your job into three parts, in rough words. Do not measure.
   - The large part: knowing the data and the problem.
   - The middle part: tools you already have. Calendar, spreadsheet, email.
   - The small part: the assistant.
2. Write what the small part does, in one line.
3. Name the fact in your own job that prose cannot fix. The one that lives in a diary, a
   price list, a rota, a stock sheet.
4. Name its home.
5. Say whether the assistant can read that home today. Yes, no, or only if exported.
6. Read the three parts to your pair. Ask: "Would any of this still be work without the
   assistant?" That is the large and the middle part.

## Write down

- The three parts.
- The fact, its home, and whether the assistant can read it.

## Remember

- The split is a rough guide. Nobody has measured it. It exists to show when you started at
  the small part.
